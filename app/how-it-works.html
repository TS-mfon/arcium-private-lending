How it works

Public solvency, private borrower texture.

The lender still gets an auditable account trail, but the user keeps the sensitive credit draft in a local workspace instead of treating the browser as a plain public filing cabinet.

Operating logic

  1. Borrow actions mint obligation accounts on the deployed program.
  2. Repay, withdraw, and liquidation steps emit wallet-signed receipts.
  3. The interface decodes the resulting account and shows it back to the user.

What stays local

  1. Repayment intent and planning data remain in the browser workspace.
  2. The visible chain object is the proof of action, not the whole private context.
  3. This keeps the workflow usable without advertising every financial move.
